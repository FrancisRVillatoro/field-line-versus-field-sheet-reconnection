The original twenty-four configurations comprise: nine runs with three original amplitudes and three resolutions at $\nu=0.15$; six runs at $N=97$ with $\nu=0.30,0.075$; three larger-domain runs; three fourth-order spatial controls at $\eps=0.08$; one negative-time run; one flat run; and one run with 512 boundary points. The supplementary twenty-one configurations comprise: twelve runs at the original amplitudes adding $N=65,\nu=0.075$, $N=129,\nu=0.075$, $N=129,\nu=0.30$, and independent shooting at the reference resolution; six runs at amplitudes $0.01,0.02$ for matched spatial and time comparisons; and three runs producing the near-event slices. The finer tracing step reuses those slices and is not counted as another evolution.

The archived data retain the original cluster inputs and outputs, supplementary configurations and outputs, and an explicit inventory of the selected supplementary cases. Exploratory failed initial-profile attempts are not counted as completed evolutions. The original analysis outputs are preserved for provenance; the observable tables in this paper use the supplementary scientific evaluation and its documented indicators. Computational completion alone is not used as evidence of scientific correctness.
